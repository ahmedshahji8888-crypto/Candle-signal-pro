Signal rules & no-trade filters

  • CALL: EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21 on a closed candle and RSI is 50–68.
  • PUT: EMA 9 crosses below EMA 21 on a closed candle and RSI is 32–50.
  • WAIT: insufficient history, no fresh crossover, RSI outside the filter, or invalid data.
  • Never use a still-forming candle. CSV data must contain completed candles.
  • Do not treat these rules as a proven edge. Test on data not used to choose the rules, and use a demo environment only.
